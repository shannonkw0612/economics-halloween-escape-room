The Five Trade Mysteries • Independent Chromebook Escape Room

Midnight is coming...

A strange spell has locked five gates across Halloween Town! The only way out is to solve mysteries about European trade. Search each location by clicking objects, answer three challenges, and collect three secret digits.

But beware: the digits are NOT given in the order needed for the lock. Search for the ordering clue and arrange them yourself. Then look around Mrs. Wallace's real classroom to solve the final counting riddle for each gate.

🕯️ Every question has one best answer. Read the explanation if you miss one, then try again. All the information you need is inside the game.

Click all three objects to investigate. Your digits appear after each solved challenge.
